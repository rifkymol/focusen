# Focusen privacy policy

Focusen collects nothing. Everything it stores stays in your browser's extension storage on your device: the address, title and timer of each tab you choose to track, plus your Done and Expired lists. It reads the length and playback position of the YouTube video in a tracked tab from the page itself, so that the timer can match the video and a reopened video resumes where you left off. Focusen has no servers, accounts, analytics or third-party services, and it never sends, sells or shares any data. Uninstalling the extension deletes all of it.
